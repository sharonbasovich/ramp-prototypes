# BorrowFirst — 30-second booth demo

Script assumes `npm start` is running and the app is open at
http://localhost:5313 (chip reads **SQLite server sandbox**). If someone
asks about the static Pages build, point at the **Browser sandbox** chip
instead and say the label is the honesty guarantee.

## 0:00–0:08 — The hook

> "Your company is about to buy equipment it already owns. Watch."

State the request on screen: **3 monitors, Waterloo office, Friday, 24",
HDMI** → *Find available equipment*.

## 0:08–0:16 — The numbers

Point at the Review panel:

> "Buying all new would cost **$675**. The engine found two owned spares —
> a $15 transfer each — plus one purchase at $225. Proposed **$255**.
> **$420 of potential spending avoided** — potential, not claimed savings."

Note aloud that M-204 is amber: *owner confirmation required* — the plan is
conditional, and the reserve button stays disabled until it's confirmed.

## 0:16–0:22 — The reactivity

Change **When do you need it?** to *Tomorrow*:

> "Change one input — the remote spare can't arrive in time, the plan
> recomputes instantly, and now there's a shortage. And look — no savings
> number, because there's no feasible all-new baseline to compare against.
> We never show a number we can't back."

Set the deadline back to *Friday*.

## 0:22–0:28 — The commit

Click **Request from owner** on M-204 (it's now confirmed), then
**Confirm and reserve**:

> "One transaction reserves both assets — reservation IDs on screen, and
> the avoided spend becomes a confirmed plan. If a teammate had grabbed
> M-204 a second ago, the whole plan would bounce back for re-picking —
> no partial reservations."

## 0:28–0:30 — The close

Click **Export purchase list**:

> "Export hands procurement exactly what's left to buy plus the transfer
> instructions. Inventory in, real reservations, honest labels. That's
> BorrowFirst."
